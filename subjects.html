ECA Subject Notes

Starter notes for B.Ed. learners. Check your university syllabus for prescribed units.

TOPIC 01

Meaning and nature of ECA

Educational and Co-Curricular Activities complement classroom learning and support intellectual, social, emotional, physical and creative development.

Key points
  • Meaning and characteristics
  • Curricular and co-curricular learning
  • Scope and educational value
TOPIC 02

Aims and importance

Activities give learners opportunities to apply knowledge, cooperate, communicate and build confidence.

  • Holistic development
  • Creativity and leadership
  • Citizenship and collaboration
TOPIC 03

Types and planning

Art, music, drama, debate, sports, clubs, exhibitions, school assemblies and community projects.

  • Set learning outcomes
  • Prepare materials and roles
  • Plan inclusion and safety
TOPIC 04

Teacher's role

Teachers organise, facilitate, encourage fair participation and help learners reflect.

  • Activity coordination
  • Observation and feedback
  • Reflection and improvement

These are general introductory notes, not an official university syllabus. Add your course units and prescribed readings as needed.
